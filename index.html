Fic Archive Catalog

Hello. This is a filterable and searchable catalog of fanfiction. Fics contained in this catalog have not necessarily been read or endorsed by fogsrollingin, as fogs often downloads after a page or two of reading, then may abandon the fic. You can find fogsrollingin's neocities website, and within, her fics are available to download directly, and her fic recommendations are there too. To look at all the inner workings of this project, visit its github page. This GitHub page should not be scraped or indexed by search engines; if you're here it's by word of mouth inside fandom circles.
